# Compliance — Strengths, gaps and synergies through the lens of the Mid-Term Plan
### Head of Compliance — Brief for the Direction

**Date:** 13 September 2026
**Basis:** Only what is legible in the photos in this folder (converted `.HEIC` → `./JPEG/`). No external facts added.
**Slides:** `MTP PLAN.jpg` + 12 units: GMRC (`IMG_0075`), FS (`IMG_0076`), ICBRC (`IMG_0079`), EAG (`IMG_0080`), Central Compliance (`IMG_0081`), STP (`IMG_0082`), CDAO (`IMG_0083`), Americas (`IMG_0084`), APAC (`IMG_0085`), CASPL Hub (`IMG_0086`), MEA (`IMG_0087` = `IMG_0088`), UK (`IMG_0089`). All team slides: "Preparation of the 2027 CPL Budget", 6 Sept 2026.

> For executives who already know the detail: this note does not repeat every achievement. It gives a judgement per team — where it is strong, what it still needs to fix — where teams should work together, and how it all ladders up to the Mid-Term Plan.

---

## Executive snapshot

1. **Credibility is now a strength, not a remediation story.** The historic overhangs are closed in the slides (2015 order [FS], OFAC order [Americas], 11-month AMF/NFA exams [GMRC], APRA/China/HKMA/FSS closures [APAC]). The risk has moved from "will we pass inspections?" to "can we scale without losing that standing?" — AMLA by 2027, AI Act, DAC8 from 2027, SM&CR Phase 2 from 2027, algo oversight, weekly Takeover reporting.
2. **Every team now claims a business-enablement role — but maturity differs.** Americas, ICBRC, GMRC and FS can already name desks, licences and platforms they enabled (SBSD, Delta One, GMD/GRI, SEPA/Switzerland, IndoCorp path). UK, APAC and EAG describe enablement still to deliver (Takeover rebuild, GMD equities, defence initiative). That gap is the 2027-28 priority.
3. **The MTP is followed by all — but through division of labour, not by everyone doing everything.** Central + CDAO + STP/CASPL carry Cohesion and Transformation (one programme, one hub, one data/AI backbone). Regions and business-line teams carry Acceleration (advisory, de-risking, regulator handling). This is the right split; the weakness is at the joints (data feeds, model monitoring, Level 1/Level 2 transfers, MyPACT roll-out) where one team's ambition depends on another team's delivery.
4. **Efficiency will come from reuse, not new builds.** The same needs appear 3-4 times (surveillance tuning, KYC enrichment, training controls/exemptions, risk-assessment collection, AI assistants). The plan already names the shared answers (Mumbai/CASPL, SONAR/Shield/DARK, MyPACT, CDH/ACE/Nucleus, AIDA/Compass, KYC Cure/COMPASS, DARE method). The Direction's leverage is to forbid local rebuilds and arbitrate the two "to be studied" items together (FS/AI teams at CASPL; CBK transfer for L2 filtering [STP]).

## 1. The Mid-Term Plan in three sentences

Source: `MTP PLAN.jpg`.

- **Accelerate:** be part of strategic decisions — trusted advisory as differentiator, controls focused where business and licences require (de-risk), proactive regulator handling.
- **Cohesion:** one global programme ("one global rule book"), secured expertise, empowered teams that attract talent.
- **Transform:** one offshore platform in India, AI on industrialised processes (with explicit warning on model cost), pooled data (KRI, Risk Assessment), simpler training/tools/procedures.

Mission reminder on the slide: protect the Bank, its clients and staff, while ensuring sustainable growth under business momentum and regulatory pressure.

## 2. Team-by-team: strengths and what still needs to improve

Read as: **Strength = proven in 2026. To improve = implied by the team's own 2027-28 ambitions (i.e. not yet done, or dependent on others).**

### GMRC — Markets surveillance and cross-border [IMG_0075]
**Strength:** proven ability to scale surveillance under exam pressure — 4 new countries, 6 languages, new models, fewer false positives, while passing AMF/NFA scrutiny. Mumbai hub already used as scale lever. Cross-border and EU-office frameworks delivered as reusable packs (tool + country sheets + training).
**To improve:** the scale is not yet industrialised. End-to-end data governance, new GMD models and Shield coverage are listed as 2027-28 work; Product Governance and Best Execution still need review to support distribution growth; regulatory-reporting remediation depends on CMR (outside CPL). Risk if Mumbai capacity and data lineage do not keep pace with GMD/GRI growth.

### FS — Financial crime, KYC, sanctions [IMG_0076]
**Strength:** the Bank's biggest credibility win (2015 order closure, NY rating upgrade). SONAR coverage finished (Brazil, France/CIB, foreign-currency clearing, SEPA Instant) and KYC made genuinely risk-based (routine to first line, only high-risk to FS; shift to dynamic reviews Q3-Q4 2026). First European data collection for future AMLA supervision already run.
**To improve:** the 2027-28 plan is heavy and sequential — Trade Finance (2027) then Capital Markets (data 2027, live 2028), continuous KYC on the Data Hub, AI-embedded opinions, Fraud & Corruption assessment (2027/28), AMLA solution by 2027, correspondent-banking coverage (IP CBK). Almost every item depends on data enrichment and the shared data hub. Risk is sequencing and bandwidth, not intent.

### ICBRC — Conflicts and personal dealings, investment-banking advisory [IMG_0079]
**Strength:** clean execution on a tight regulatory deadline (new PAD framework + MyPACT April 2026, all AMF remediation by June 2026), plus governance clean-up (conflicts report, inducements, texts, control plan) and credible support to IndoCorp (expert + officer + weekly presence). Training refreshed jointly with HR/business.
**To improve:** still head-office-centric. "More global" (NYC ties, global training/control plan/newsletter) and automation (Restricted List, Credit Research, insider lists + email surveillance) are ambitions, not achievements. MyPACT must still go to EU (Q1 2027), APAC (2028), possibly CACEIS; IndoCorp's regulated status is a 2-year path (apply Q1 2027, obtain Q3 2028). Needs STP/CASPL and regional adoption to succeed.

### EAG — ESG, privacy, tax, culture, whistleblowing [IMG_0080]
**Strength:** breadth with evidence — ESG at 85% + 87 opinions/45 contributions in 7 months, 100% of 2024 privacy audit recommendations closed, local privacy officers appointed, tax non-compliant stock reduced, 6 new training controls, whistleblowing handled on time with stronger rating process shared with the regulator.
**To improve:** several "method done, execution pending" items: data purge method built (DARE) but purge still to execute in CPL then business lines; tax digital collection studied but DAC8 live from 2027; Code of Conduct still to issue (end 2026); ESG still to scale with AI/digital and embed in KYC; defence-sector MTP initiative still to support; Themis/BKMS automation still to deliver. Classic spread-too-thin risk — needs sequencing and shared automation.

### Central Compliance — People, cohesion, transformation engine [IMG_0081]
**Strength:** the only team whose 2026 work is all about others' capacity — Inspire (trusted-advisor skills), HR training, expert certification, new intranet/townhall/newsletter/pitch, CRA rollout started, NAP simplification, budget tool to ExCom, AI framework, KYC Cure/COMPASS, new analytics base (ACE on CDH), CASPL teams supervised.
**To improve:** most transformation items are "launched", not "finished": CRA digitalisation + full 2027 exercise, CARE replacement + new opinion templates, dashboard migration to CDH, NAP/complaints/regulator-assignment optimisation, control-framework assessment, AI training team at CASPL, AI agents for model monitoring, silo-breaking/brand work. Its credibility in 2027 depends on finishing these shared utilities on time.

### STP — Transversal projects and offshoring blueprint [IMG_0082]
**Strength:** delivered the organisational moves others talk about — India hub growth (QA, surveillance, monitoring), MyPact in France + stronger personal-dealing controls, IndoCorp top-risk upgrade (P0/P1), 6 AMF training controls with clear roles.
**To improve:** the 2027-28 plan is a list of completions owned jointly: MyPact Europe, IndoCorp P2/P3, Level 1 centralisation in GRM hubs including Americas, L2 filtering reinforcement. Two scale decisions are explicitly "to be studied" (new FS/AI teams at CASPL; CBK transfer from CASA DDC). Leaving them undecided blocks FS, GMRC, MEA and APAC plans that assume the hub.

### CDAO — Data & AI Office [IMG_0083]
**Strength:** speed and traction — capability built in under 6 months (6 FTE +1 in Q4 +5 consultants), single AI governance (AIDA) with 36+ initiatives governed and Green AI Act readiness rating, COMEX-sponsored Data Governance, product delivery (65 releases, 12+ projects), 120+ officers upskilled in days, "commando" value (AMF reporting, AMLA dry run), first Augmented Officer case for end 2026.
**To improve:** small relative to demand — targeted hiring still needed. Global AIDA deployment, Compass upgrade, international Data Governance, automated quality/monitoring, production-grade platform with SLAs and self-service on "trusted" data, and scaled GenAI are all 2027-28 ambitions. Every other team's AI plan assumes CDAO delivers validation, monitoring and training capacity.

### Americas [IMG_0084]
**Strength:** the most complete MTP showcase in one region — OFAC closure + Satisfactory audit ratings + SBSD/Delta One business wins + "One Compliance" governance + US reorganisation (bigger Advisory, centralised model management) + process review removing duplication + stronger Regulatory Watch (Treasury Clearing Rule).
**To improve:** the shift from one-off clean-up to repeatable scale: faster product onboarding with automated monitoring, oversight model across local + offshore, streamlined risk assessment/controls, standardisation with Head Office, tech-integrated operating model (swap-marketing workflow, registration upgrade, AI in operations), overlap/resource optimisation. All are ambitions — the test is whether the reorganisation sustains excellence without the enforcement urgency.

### APAC excl. CASPL [IMG_0085]
**Strength:** stability in a difficult context — 4 regulators managed to closure/validation (APRA, China to 2B, HKMA, FSS), global tools adopted (SONAR/DARK/Shield), controls centralised at CASPL, team stability (low turnover, trainee/intern pipeline, exchanges).
**To improve:** structural constraints remain: geopolitics/sanctions (US-China), recurring inspections, Cloud and cross-border data limits that directly constrain AIDA/AI ambitions, swap-dealer requirements in 3 hubs still to implement, GMD equities support still to deliver, Japan hiring and Korea capacity gaps. Most dependent of all regions on CASPL + CDAO + Head Office choices.

### CASPL CPL Hub — India platform [IMG_0086]
**Strength:** the MTP's offshore pillar made tangible — 6 FTE (2024) → 26 → 40 (2026) → 62 (2027) → 75 (2028), with quality (QA 9→22) and model testing (new unit 4→5) built in, not added after. Gives the Direction a quantified scale story.
**To improve:** execution risk of adding ~35 people in 2 years while preserving quality, plus indecision on FS/AI teams. Also a dependency risk in reverse: if Head Office processes (CRA, surveillance tuning, L1/L2 definitions) are not standardised first, the hub industrialises fragmentation.

### MEA [IMG_0087]
**Strength:** resilience under real stress — business continuity during conflict (staff in other jurisdictions), compliance risks contained, extra regulator queries answered, business sustained; governance changes absorbed (Arabia/QFC officers, withdrawals/applications); DFSA/UAE inspections well handled; controls already moving to CASPL (CASTORE).
**To improve:** leans heavily on two transfers still in flight — to CASPL (since H2 2026) and to the first line (KEOP empowerment) — plus early-stage AI helpers (Alfred, Copilot). Rising scrutiny (biennial QFC IGE, QFCRA Nov 2026) leaves little slack. Needs the hub and first-line empowerment to work, not just to be planned.

### UK [IMG_0089]
**Strength:** pragmatic, business-aware delivery — retargeted training (weekly front-office questions surfacing real gaps), SONAR live on more payment types, Phase 1 SM&CR/NFM landed with HR/Legal, personal-dealing and gifts rules rewritten to reduce burden while staying risk-based.
**To improve:** the team itself flags control gaps (notably trade including algos — self-identified + internal-audit recommendations) requiring remediation; local AI governance demanded by UK regulators still to build; Takeover reporting must be rebuilt with Paris as volumes jumped from every-other-month to weekly on equities growth (Amundi/DPAM); Phase 2 SM&CR/NFM from 2027 expands CPL's adjudication role; London needs tool access + AI upskilling; alert volumes with tighter deadlines strain capacity. Growth-conditional agenda.

## 3. Where the real interactions are — 6 joint plays

Each play names who must work together and the efficiency at stake. All items are cited in the source slides; the judgement is where pooling beats parallel work.

**Play 1 — One surveillance factory (scale without linear hiring).**
GMRC (models, Shield, Mumbai) + CASPL/Mumbai capacity + UK + APAC (SONAR volumes, tighter deadlines) + Central (COMPASS / CARE successor) + CDAO (data, validation, monitoring).
Gain: one tuning/false-positive discipline, one capacity plan (languages, countries, payment channels), one lineage from payment to alert to case. Without it, UK alert surge and GMRC's 4-country/6-language extension each buy their own capacity.

**Play 2 — One client-data and KYC backbone (enrich once, reuse everywhere).**
FS (dynamic/360°/real-time KYC, portfolio reviews, Trade Finance/Capital Markets data) + CDAO/Central (CDH/ACE, KYC Cure) + EAG (ESG-in-KYC, purge method DARE, tax-document digitalisation, FATCA/DAC8).
Gain: enrich clients/accounts once, reuse for AML, ESG, tax and risk scoring; purge what must go on the same platform that enriches what must stay. Without it, enrichment and purge work against each other and business lines are asked twice.

**Play 3 — One AI governance and model-operations chain (pay validation once).**
CDAO (AIDA, Compass, Augmented Officer) + Central (industrial-AI framework, AI agent for model monitoring, AI team at CASPL, Model Training & Testing Unit 4→5 FTE) + FS/GMRC (opinions, fraud/market-anomaly, GMD models, false positives) + UK/MEA/APAC (local AI governance, Alfred/Copilot, Cloud/data constraints).
Gain: shared validation/monitoring/training instead of each team funding model cost (explicit MTP warning). CDAO's 4 trainings/120+ officers is the template — replicate, don't rebuild; give London/regions access to approved assistants.

**Play 4 — One conduct and advisory system (same rules, same training, same brand).**
ICBRC (MyPACT, Restricted List, insider lists, global training/newsletter, NYC link) + STP (MyPact France→Europe, AMF training controls) + Central (Inspire, E-ADN, intranet/brand, exemption/deployment automation) + EAG (Code of Conduct, 6 training controls, Scan'Ups, MMLC) + UK (SM&CR/NFM, weekly questions) + Americas/MEA (Trusted Advisor / proactive partner).
Gain: frameworks + tool + training + control built once (cross-border sheets, EU-office pack, IndoCorp P0/P1→P2/P3 as templates), MyPACT rolled EU Q1 2027 → APAC 2028 → possibly CACEIS instead of local tools, one exemption/tracking control proving completion.

**Play 5 — One risk-assessment and indicator system (one collection, many uses).**
Central (full 2027 CRA, KRI analysis, 2.2 controls, materials-analysis function) + FS (AML/Sanctions industrialisation, Fraud & Corruption 2027/28, convergence to overall CRA, AMLA design) + Americas (streamlined assessment) + CDAO (trusted/audit-ready data) + GMRC (end-to-end governance).
Gain: one calendar/collection/action-tracking feeding AMLA, AI Act, regional assessments and inspection follow-up (past-due recommendations are an explicit MEA/UK metric). Without it, first line answers the same question four times.

**Play 6 — Finish the operating-model move (decide the last centralisations together).**
STP/CASPL (L1 in GRM hubs incl. Americas; L2 filtering reinforcement) + MEA (CASTORE) + APAC (branch support) + Americas (offshore leverage) + FS (L1/L2 split; CBK perimeter from CASA DDC) + Central (supervision/training of hub).
Gain: handle Takeover-weekly, SONAR-volume and multilingual-surveillance growth at lower unit cost with QA built in (QA to 20-22 FTE). Requires the two "to be studied" decisions to be taken jointly with CASA, not team by team.

## 4. How they all follow the Mid-Term Plan — and where it is thin

**Accelerate (business partner + de-risk + regulators): followed by all, strongest where a business outcome is named.**
Advisory differentiator is most credible in ICBRC (dedicated team, industry forums), Americas (expanded Advisory, faster onboarding ambition) and MEA (on-the-ground GMD support); Central's Inspire is the cross-cutting enabler. De-risking ("licence + business only") is most visible in GMRC (product/Best Execution reviews), FS (risk-based KYC, portfolio scoring), UK (pragmatic PAD/gifts rewrite) and MEA (KEOP first-line empowerment). Proactive regulator handling is a collective strength in 2026; 2027-28 keeps it (AMLA, AI Act Green to defend, QFCRA/IGE/DFSA/UAE cycle, UK local AI governance, FSS/APRA/HKMA continuity).

**Cohesion (one programme + expertise + talent): mid-journey, carried by Central/CDAO/STP.**
One programme is proven where a single tool won (SONAR/DARK/Shield, AIDA, MyPACT in France) and pending where roll-out is scheduled (MyPACT EU/APAC, AIDA global, Data Governance international, ICBRC global control plan, Americas-HO standardisation). Expertise is secured at the centre (CDAO <6-month build, CASPL QA/model unit) but thin locally (Japan hiring, Korea capacity, London AI skills, IndoCorp depth). Talent/empowerment is the thinnest MTP sub-theme — only FS (LOD1), MEA (KEOP) and Central (Inspire/E-ADN/Job Discovery) name it explicitly; other teams still frame training as completion, not empowerment.

**Transform (India + AI/data pooling + simpler processes): the main 2027-28 bet, foundations laid in 2026.**
India is quantified and on track (CASPL timeline) but its last mile is undecided (FS/AI teams, CBK/L2). AI/data pooling has governance (AIDA), product cadence (Nucleus 65 releases) and first value (AMF reporting, AMLA dry run, false-positive cuts, Augmented Officer pilot) — scaling to production-grade platform/SLAs/self-service is the 2027-28 test, under APAC data-localisation and UK local-oversight constraints. Simplification (NAP, NSU tool, 8 privacy texts, standard templates, Themis move, CARE replacement) is real but scattered — needs one process-simplification backlog owned centrally or gains stay local.

**Bottom line for the Direction:** no team is off-plan. The imbalance to correct is: strong on Acceleration-exams and Transformation-foundations, lighter on Cohesion-empowerment and on finishing shared utilities (CRA digitalisation, CARE successor, CDH dashboards, L1/L2, MyPACT roll-out). Those shared utilities are exactly the synergies in Section 3.

## 5. What to say and decide in the Direction meeting

**Message:** "2026 restored credibility and proved compliance can enable growth. 2027-28 is about scaling that without scaling cost — one hub, one data/AI backbone, one rule book. The plans already align to the MTP; what we need from the Direction is to enforce reuse and arbitrate shared capacity."

**4 decisions to put on the table (all within what the slides propose):**
1. Confirm CASPL to 75 FTE and decide the two "to be studied" items in one go with CASA (FS/AI teams; CBK/L2 filtering) [STP/CASPL].
2. Mandate single source (CDH/ACE/Nucleus) for the 2027 full CRA, KRI and all regulatory reporting; no parallel extracts [Central/FS/CDAO/MTP].
3. Mandate reuse over rebuild: MyPACT EU→APAC, SONAR/Shield/DARK tuning, KYC Cure/COMPASS/CARE successor, DARE purge method, common training/exemption block, approved AI assistants [ICBRC/STP/FS/EAG/Central/UK/CDAO].
4. Ask for two consolidated views for the next Direction review: (a) one surveillance + KYC + risk-assessment roadmap with shared Mumbai/GRM capacity; (b) one business-enablement dashboard (licences, desks, lead-times: SBSD, Delta One, GMD/GRI/equities, SEPA/Switzerland, swap-dealer HK/JP/SG, IndoCorp status, Takeover volumes).
